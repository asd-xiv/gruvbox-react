# 04a. Square the corners under the shadow

Changes [04. Cast one Turbo Pascal shadow](04.cast-turbo-pascal-shadow.md).

**Date**: 2026-11-09

**Problem**:

- The shadow has square corners
- `Callout` rounded its corners 4px, `Button` 2px. The page showed between the
  rounded bottom-right corner and the shadow

**Solution**:

- A component with the shadow composes no `rounded-*` utility: `Button` and
  `Callout`

**Acknowledgments**:

- A ghost or a link `Button` casts no shadow, but loses its 2px corners too. All
  variants keep one shape
