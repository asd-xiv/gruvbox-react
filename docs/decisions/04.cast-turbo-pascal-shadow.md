# 04. Cast one Turbo Pascal shadow

**Date**: 2026-11-08

**Problem**:

- `--gvb-shadow-sm`, `--gvb-shadow-md` and `--gvb-shadow-lg` defined blurred
  shadows in an `rgba` black
  - The README states "No transparencies or derivative colors"
- With three sizes, the author of each component had to pick one. Turbo Pascal
  drew one shadow

**Solution**:

- Cast one shadow, 8px right and 8px down, with no blur, as Turbo Pascal drew it
  - Black (`dark0-hard`) on the light scheme, gray (`dark3`) on the dark one.
    `dark0-hard` barely shows on the dark page
- Dots of the shadow color fake its transparency, as Diablo did: a 2x2 PNG per
  scheme, in `--gvb-shadow-dither`
  - Each pixel is the shadow color or empty, so the browser never blends two
    colors
- The focused window, and every component inside it, casts the shadow solid. For
  now, the focused window is a modal dialog
- A modal dialog dims the page behind it with the same dots
- One `shadow` utility draws the shadow. A component composes it, eg. a filled
  `Button` or a `Callout`
  - To remove the shadow from a component, remove one word: `shadow` in its
    `composes`
- Remove the three `--gvb-shadow-*` tokens and the `.shadow-*` utilities

**Acknowledgments**:

- The utility draws the shadow outside the box, where an `overflow` on the
  component would clip it. A child scrolls, never the component
- A component with the shadow has a 1px border. The utility draws in the
  component's `::after`, so the component cannot use it
